Why Consistency Is Important for Students

Author: Amit

Published: September 25, 2026

Consistency is one of the most important habits a student can develop.

Studying for one or two hours every day can be easier to maintain than studying for ten hours once a week. Small improvements eventually become significant improvements.

My Advice

  1. Set a small daily goal.
  2. Work on it every day.
  3. Track your progress.
  4. Do not worry about perfection.
